```text
The EngMon workbook for quarter one — the paper version of the weekly English magazine.

WHAT IS IN IT
· Travel English, work English, everyday conversation and news English — every published week.

· 48 quiz items, every one with an explanation of the answer.

WHAT THE WORKBOOK ADDS
· Work through the quiz and dictation first, then mark them with the separate answer key.

THE LISTENING PACK (MP3)
· Every dictation sentence, every conversation line and every example sentence as audio.
· Conversations move between an American and a British accent, so your ear gets both.
· The same files on every device, and they play offline.
· These voices are synthesised, not recorded by a person.

YOU GET THE WHOLE QUARTER
Four weeks are ready today. As the rest of quarter one is finished, the same file is updated
and you can download it again at no extra cost.

ALSO WORTH KNOWING
Everything stays free to read at engmon.monster. The workbook is the version you can fill in
and mark on paper — it does not lock anything away.

REFUNDS
Ask within seven days of downloading and you get your money back.
```

## 4. 사이트에 붙일 때

1. `index.html` 의 `#buy` 안 **`data-shop-url=""` 한 곳**에 상품 링크를 넣습니다.
2. 그 순간 표지의 구매 버튼과 구매 화면이 함께 나타납니다(비어 있으면 둘 다 숨겨집니다).
3. `npm test` 로 확인합니다 — 링크가 한 곳인지, 빈 값일 때 숨겨지는지, 새 창·`noopener` 가 붙는지 검사합니다.
